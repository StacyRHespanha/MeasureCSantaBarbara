  • What it is: the State Street Master Plan (SSMP) lists two ways to borrow $60–70M for construction (Plan p. 177). Option 1 is a lease revenue bond repaid from Measure C, which Council can approve without a public vote. Option 2 is a general obligation bond, which needs two-thirds voter approval and is repaid by a property tax. This toggle adds Option 1.
  • Annual cost: about $3.7–4.5M a year for 30 years, drawn at the $4.1M midpoint. This is my calculation using the City's own assumptions for General Fund-backed borrowing: 4.5% interest, 30 years, level payments, and 4% issuance costs (NHA Advisors, Finance Committee, Feb 24, 2026, p. 16). For comparison, the City's 2024 police station bonds cost about 3.9%, which would put a $65M bond at about $3.9M a year.
  • Timing: payments start in FY2028, consistent with the Plan's call for construction to begin within 18 months of its adoption on Sept 1, 2026. The first year's payment could be smaller, or deferred if interest is borrowed during construction, as with the police station bonds.
